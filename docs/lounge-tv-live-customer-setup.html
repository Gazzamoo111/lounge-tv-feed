Get watching in a few steps.

Your Lounge TV Live channel list is delivered through Nuvio + IPTV. Your movies, series and other Nuvio add-ons are managed separately.

1 Open Nuvio + IPTV

Use the supported Nuvio + IPTV version provided with your welcome instructions. Open your normal Nuvio profile.

2 Add a Live TV source

Go to the IPTV/Live TV source settings. Select Add playlist and choose M3U URL, not Xtream Codes.

3 Add your personal link

Paste the unique Lounge TV Live playlist link supplied privately when your account was activated. Name it Lounge TV Live, then save.

There is no shared public playlist URL. Do not post your individual link publicly or forward it to another household.

4 Refresh Live TV

Allow the channel list and programme guide to finish loading. Open 01 New Zealand and try a channel, then confirm another channel and the guide.

5 Start watching

Browse the numbered categories. The final 99 Sports Event Sources section contains additional sports and alternative feeds and is normally not needed for everyday browsing.

TVNZ 1 and TVNZ 2: Keep the playlist in M3U mode. Changing it to an Xtream login may lose the playback information used by protected TVNZ channels.

Having trouble?

First refresh the playlist, then restart Nuvio. If something still fails, tell Lounge TV support the channel name, device, approximate time and any on-screen error. Never send your password or full playlist link in a public forum.

Subscription and activation enquiries: loungetv.nz. Lounge TV Live requires an active account. Access and supported features may vary by device.
