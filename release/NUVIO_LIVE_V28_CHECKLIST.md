## Delivery contract
- Lounge TV Live is a **Live TV M3U** source within **Nuvio + IPTV** on Fire TV, Android TV and Google TV.
- Movies/series and third-party add-ons remain separately configured in Nuvio. Do not market TorBox or other providers' subscriptions as bundled until commercial terms are explicitly authorised.
- Use an individual revocable link generated by the existing `lounge-customer-playlist-qa` token gateway **only after a paid/approved account has been assigned**. Do not publish shared BEST credentials or an account-bearing URL in a website or APK.
- Source contract: `GET /playlist/<recipient-token>`, paired EPG `/epg/<recipient-token>`, and token-checked TVNZ licence relay. A customer must import via **M3U**, not Xtream.
- One customer's link/credential must not authenticate as another customer. Revoke access on expiry/cancellation. The existing QA gateway is a testing implementation, not yet customer-accepted.
